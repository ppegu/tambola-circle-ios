Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: a high-fidelity UX concept board for the existing Tambola Circle mobile app, showing FOUR complete portrait mobile screens side by side, ordered left to right, with small numbered captions above them. Generate one wide, very high resolution raster image, approximately 3840 by 2160. Each screen must be fully visible with enough room for readable interface text. Flat straight-on UI presentation, no angled devices, no hands.

Primary request: Design the voice settings flow before implementation. Aria is the female default voice bundled inside both Android and iOS apps. The other four female voices (Neerja, Ava, Emma, Sonia) are optional downloads, stored on the device for subsequent offline use. The user can preview, download, and explicitly select a voice. Downloading never switches the active voice automatically.

Visual style of the CURRENT APP to match faithfully: rich deep purple background (#28054b) with subtle radial violet glow and restrained tiny gold sparkles near the edges, large rounded warm cream cards (#fff0ce), white and gold card borders with a dark gold lower edge for slight raised dimension, bold friendly rounded typography, dark purple text. Circular glossy purple back buttons with white chevrons. Primary buttons are glossy warm golden yellow with brown text and soft dimensional bottom shadow. Secondary preview actions are turquoise, positive badges and selection checkmarks green. This is a polished, friendly family Tambola game, not a corporate dashboard. Keep ornamental graphics subtle and keep content extremely legible. Small abstract sound-wave badges rather than invented portraits of named voices. Consistent status bars 9:41, no device mockup hardware. Outer presentation board a very pale warm cream so the purple screens stand out. Compact brand text "TAMBOLA CIRCLE" at top of board; headline "Choose your caller voice". Small footer beneath screens: "UX concept · Download sizes are illustrative".
