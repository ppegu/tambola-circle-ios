Game V4 · Simple audio toggles

One small mic and one small speaker. Tap either to toggle muted/unmuted directly. No overlay, picker or menu. Design proposal; voice chat is not yet implemented.

Review and implementation plan · Image prompts

Mic defaults muted. Unmute lets everyone listening at the table hear you. Speaker mutes/unmutes all incoming voice and number announcements, restoring saved levels. Both controls are independent.
